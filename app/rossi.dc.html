La nostra storia

Good food doesn’t need explaining

Rossi opened in 1987 with six tables, one pasta machine, and a recipe for ragù that Marco Rossi had carried from Bologna in his coat pocket.

That ragù is still on the menu. So is the pasta machine.

We’ve grown since then: a larger kitchen, a wine cellar, a team we’re proud of. But the logic hasn’t changed. Source the best ingredients we can find. Cook them properly. Serve them with care.

Come hungry.

What we believe in

Three things we take seriously

The ingredients

We drive to the market every morning. Our olive oil comes from a single estate in Puglia. Our pasta flour from a mill in Emilia-Romagna.

The craft

Everything on the menu is made by hand. The pasta, the bread, the stocks. We don’t use shortcuts because shortcuts show up on the plate.

The room

A meal at Rossi should feel unhurried. Our team knows when to pour wine and when to disappear. The evening is yours.
