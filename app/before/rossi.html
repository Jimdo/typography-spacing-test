Good food doesn’t need explaining

Rossi opened in 1987 with six tables, one pasta machine, and a recipe for ragù that Marco Rossi had carried from Bologna in his coat pocket.
 That ragù is still on the menu. So is the pasta machine. We've grown since then — a larger kitchen, a wine cellar, a team we're proud of — but the logic hasn't changed. Source the best ingredients we can find. Cook them properly. Serve them with care.
